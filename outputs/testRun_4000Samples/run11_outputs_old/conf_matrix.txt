------------ FORMATO DA MATRIZ --------
----------------[TN, FP]------------------
----------------[FN, TP]------------------

TN: predicted Negative, Reality Negative
FN: predicted Negative, Reality Positive
FP: predicted Positive, Reality Negative
TP: predicted Positive, Reality Positive

------------ METRICAS --------
precision: TP / (TP + FP) 
recall: TP / (TP + FN) 
f1: 2 * precision * recall / (precision + recall)
